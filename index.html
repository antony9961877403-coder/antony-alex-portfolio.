The Intersect of Design & Logic

I am Antony Alex, a creative designer based in Kerala, India. My path in design is shaped by an organic curiosity for how aesthetics connect with logic.

With a background in Computer Science (B.Sc.), I don’t view design as merely decorative surface work. Instead, I appreciate visual systems the way an engineer appreciates sound architecture: balanced, systematic, scalable, and built for real human interaction.

During my one year at a marketing agency, I tackled fast-paced creative briefs, translated business goals into striking visual assets, and delivered campaigns tailored for distinct audiences.

Today, I’m deeply passionate about vibe coding—using modern AI-driven velocity and rapid prototyping to turn graphic design ideas into living, interactive digital artifacts without friction.
